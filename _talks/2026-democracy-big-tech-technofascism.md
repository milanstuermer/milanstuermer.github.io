At [Democracy: Dead or Alive?](https://arminius.nl/programma/democracy-dead-or-alive/), I discussed technofascism with Eva Rovers and Stan Boeve, drawing on materials collected for the recently published special issue [*Technofascism*](https://doi.org/10.25819/ubsi/11107) that I have co-edited with Laura Hille and Felix Hüttemann.
