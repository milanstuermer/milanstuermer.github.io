Drawing on Isabelle Stengers’s call to civilize modernity, I asked what it would mean to civilize AI and the abstractions it produces. I brought Whitehead’s account of civilization as transmission that preserves freedom for successors into dialogue with Lewis Mumford’s critique of the megamachine, focusing on the abstractions that govern contemporary LLM architectures.
