I reinterpret Bernard Stiegler’s late neganthropological project through Whitehead’s concept of reason, tracing the role of Nicholas Georgescu-Roegen’s bio-physio-economics in this encounter. The article connects technology, ecology, and economy through reason as a capacity to orient novelty toward living better, and reads the Neganthropocene as a programme for sociotherapy.
